# APPENDIX: VOICE & STYLISTIC NOTES

## Tone Summary
- **Write like a person, not a brand.** Use "we," never "the company."
- **Be honest about being early.** Don't hide that it's a pre-launch. Lean into it — early = exclusive.
- **Lead with feeling, not features.** The features are in the copy. The headlines should trigger emotion.
- **No jargon, no fluff.** No "leverage synergies." No "disrupting travel."
- **Respect the reader's intelligence.** You don't need to explain why connection matters. They already know. You're just building the tool to make it easier.
- **Be warm but not desperate.** We're excited, not needy.
- **Community language:** "early explorer," "founding member," "early community" — not "early adopter" (too tech-bro).

## Color Direction
*(Suggested, not mandated)*
- **Primary:** Deep indigo or charcoal gray
- **Accent:** Warm coral, terracotta, or electric teal
- **Background:** Off-white sections alternate with a dark section to create visual rhythm
- **App vibes to reference:** Duolingo's warmth + Airbnb's editorial feel + Notion's spatial clarity

## Typography Direction
- **Headlines:** Bold, modern sans-serif. Large, high contrast. Think "impactful first, detailed second."
- **Body:** Clean, highly readable. Generous line-height. No cramped paragraphs.
- **Section labels:** Small caps or uppercase tracking — used sparingly for hierarchy.

## Layout Rhythm (Suggested)
1. **Hero** — Full-width, bold, immediate
2. **Product intro** — 3-column or 3-card, airy
3. **Why we exist** — Full-width text, personal, emotional
4. **Problem** — Card grid (2x2 or 3+2), punchy
5. **How it works** — Step-by-step or feature cards, lighter section
6. **Future features** — Icon grid, slightly muted tone (signal "coming soon")
7. **Why join early** — Warm highlights, benefit-focused, centered
8. **Vision** — Dark background, large pull quote, inspirational
9. **Founder story** — Editorial photo feel, personal layout
10. **Roadmap** — Timeline visual, clean and honest
11. **FAQ** — Accordion style, minimal
12. **Waitlist CTA** — High contrast, centered, maximum urgency and warmth
13. **Footer** — Dark, anchoring

## Brand Wordlist

**USE often:**
- Together, adventure, connection, people, journey, stories, explore, authentic, real
- "Early explorers," "founding members," "first in line"

**AVOID:**
- "Disrupt," "innovative," "game-changing," "cutting-edge"
- "Seamless," "leverage," "ecosystem," "synergy"
- Fake urgency ("LIMITED SPOTS!") unless genuinely limited
